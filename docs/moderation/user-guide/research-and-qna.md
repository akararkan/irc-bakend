# Research Papers and Q&A

Part of the [moderation user guide](README.md).

## Research papers

Your paper isn't checked while you're still drafting it — you can write,
save, and re-save a draft freely. The check happens **at the moment you hit
Publish.**

What gets checked, all together: title, abstract, body, keywords, citation
text, every tag, every source's title and citation text, every media
caption/alt-text, and every contributor's note.

- **Clean** → publishes immediately, as normal.
- **Held** → your paper stays as a draft (only visible to you) while it's
  checked, rather than a separate "pending" paper state — you'll see it in
  your drafts, and it becomes fully public the moment it clears.
- **Refused** → the publish action itself is refused; your paper stays a
  draft with everything you wrote intact, so you can revise and try
  publishing again.

**Typical timing:** clears in under 5 seconds; hard ceiling is 60 seconds
(research papers are long, so the check gets a bit more time than a post).

### Editing a published paper

Editing a **live** paper's title, abstract, body, tags, sources, or
contributor notes re-checks the changed text, the same way editing a post
does: a refused edit keeps your paper exactly as it was published; a held
edit takes the paper back to draft (hidden from readers) until the new
wording clears. Editing a draft that hasn't been published yet doesn't
trigger a check — only Publish does.

### Comments on research papers

Same as regular comments: fast (well under a second, 10-second ceiling), and
a held comment is only visible to its author until it clears.

## Questions and Answers

Questions and answers work like posts and comments:

- **A new question** — title, body, keywords, and tags are checked together.
  Typical timing under 2 seconds, 30-second ceiling.
- **An answer** (or a "reanswer"/reply to an answer) — body plus any attached
  source titles/citations and attachment captions. Same timing as questions.
- **Held** questions/answers are visible only to their author until cleared;
  **refused** ones simply aren't created, with your draft preserved.
- **Editing** a question or answer re-checks it exactly like editing a post: a
  refused edit keeps the original text, a held edit takes it private again
  briefly while the new wording clears.

Adding or editing a source's title/citation, or an attachment's caption,
*after* an answer is already live is checked instantly — accepted or refused
on the spot, no waiting state, so you'll never see one of these sitting in
"pending."

## Common questions

**"I published my paper and it disappeared — did something go wrong?"**
Most likely it's back in your drafts, being briefly re-checked. Check "My
Research" / your drafts list; if it's there, it will publish automatically
once it clears (or in the rare case of a hard refusal, it stays a draft for
you to revise).

**"Can I still edit my paper while it's a draft, without triggering a
check?"**
Yes — only hitting Publish triggers the check. Edit freely beforehand.
