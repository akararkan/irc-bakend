# Posts and Comments

Part of the [moderation user guide](README.md). Covers posts, reels, comments,
replies, shares, and the small text bits attached to them (captions, alt-text,
highlight titles).

## Creating a post

When you hit "post," your caption/text — plus location text and audio-track
name, if you added them — is checked before the post exists to anyone but
you.

- **Clean** → published instantly, exactly as before. This is what happens
  almost every time.
- **Held** → the post is created, but its status is "pending review." Only you
  can see it, in your own profile and feed. Everyone else — including your
  followers — sees nothing, as if you hadn't posted. A "Checking…" badge
  should show on your own view.
- **Refused** → nothing is created. You get an error and your draft is still
  there to edit.

**Typical timing:** clears in under 2 seconds; the hard ceiling is 30 seconds.

## Editing a post

Editing re-checks the new text — this closes a loophole where someone could
post something innocent-looking and immediately edit it into something else.

- If your **edit looks fine**, nothing changes — it updates like normal.
- If your **edit is refused**, the edit doesn't go through and your post
  keeps its original, already-approved wording. You get the same error as a
  new post; think of it as "this specific change was rejected," not "your
  post was taken down."
- If your **edit is held** (uncertain, not clearly bad), here's the important
  part: **the edited version replaces the old one right away**, and the whole
  post temporarily goes back to "only visible to you" until the edit clears.
  This is different from a refusal — your post doesn't disappear because it's
  bad, it disappears because the *new wording* needs a quick check, same as
  a brand-new post would.

In short: a flat-out bad edit is blocked and your original stays up; a
borderline edit is applied but takes your post briefly private again while
it's re-checked.

## Comments and replies

Same idea as posts, just faster — comments are high-volume and people expect
them to feel instant.

- **Typical timing:** clears in well under a second; hard ceiling is 10
  seconds.
- **Held comments** are invisible to everyone but their author — including in
  reply threads. You'll still see your own comment normally; other readers of
  the thread simply won't see it until it clears.
- **Editing a comment** works exactly like editing a post: a rejected edit
  keeps your original comment text untouched; a held edit takes the comment
  private again briefly.
- Comments don't show a visible "status" the way posts do — the hiding
  happens automatically on the server, so if a comment of yours looks like it
  "vanished," check whether you can still see it yourself. If yes, it's
  being checked, not deleted.

## Shares, highlight titles, and similar short text

Share captions and highlight titles are short, one-line bits of text with no
in-between "pending" state — they're either accepted immediately or refused
immediately. If refused, nothing is saved and you can just try different
wording.

## What you'll see if it's held

Suggested experience (this is what the app should show you):

> **Checking…**
> Your post is being checked automatically. It becomes visible to others as
> soon as it clears — usually within a few seconds.

If it takes long enough to need a person's eyes on it:

> **Under review.**
> Automatic checking took longer than expected, so a moderator is taking a
> look. It stays hidden from others until then.

And once it's decided, you'll get a notification either way — "Your post is
live" or "Your post was removed," with an appeal link on the latter.

## Common questions

**"My post disappeared right after I posted it."**
It's almost certainly held for review, not deleted — check your own profile;
if you can see it there, it's just temporarily private while it's checked.

**"I edited my post and now it's hidden again — did I get in trouble?"**
No. Editing always re-checks the new wording. If the new wording is
borderline, the post goes private again for a few seconds/minutes until it's
cleared — this is normal and not a penalty.

**"I got refused and I really didn't say anything wrong."**
It happens, especially with automated systems. Appeal from your account
settings — every appeal goes to a real person, and confirmed mistakes help
the system get better at recognizing similar cases in future.
